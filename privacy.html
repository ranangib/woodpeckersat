Privacy

This page describes what information is handled when you use WoodpeckerSAT.

Practice progress

Your quiz answers and current question are saved in local storage in your browser, separately for each practice part. This lets you return to your work on the same browser and device. This information is stored on your device, not sent to WoodpeckerSAT by the practice pages. Progress for a part is cleared when you complete that part. You can also remove it by clearing this site’s local storage or site data in your browser settings.

Analytics

Some pages use Google Analytics to measure site visits and understand how the site is used. Google may collect information such as pages visited, browser and device details, and approximate location, and may use cookies or similar technologies. The homepage and these policy pages do not load the site’s Google Analytics tag. For details about Google’s handling of data, see Google’s Privacy Policy.

Question submissions

If you submit a question through the upload page, the form asks for your email address, question type, question text, and answer choices. The form is handled by Formspree, a third-party form service, and the submission is sent to the site operator so they can respond. Information you submit is subject to Formspree’s Privacy Policy as well as this page. Email sent directly to the contact address is also received by the site operator.
