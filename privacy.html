Privacy

This page describes what information is handled when you use WoodpeckerSAT.

Practice progress

Your quiz answers and current question are saved in local storage in your browser, separately for each practice part. Your bookmarked questions, latest answered-question results, practice-day streak, and selected exam month are also stored in local storage on this browser and device. These features do not send this information to a shared database. The question of the day is selected from the published practice sets and changes by date; the focus timer only runs while the page is open and is not saved. Printable sheets are generated in your browser. Progress for a part is cleared when you complete that part. You can remove locally saved data by clearing this site’s local storage or site data in your browser settings. If using “Clear browsing data,” make sure the option for site data/cookies is selected; clearing only the browser history may not remove local storage.

Analytics

Some pages use Google Analytics to measure site visits and understand how the site is used. Google may collect information such as pages visited, browser and device details, and approximate location, and may use cookies or similar technologies. The homepage and these policy pages do not load the site’s Google Analytics tag. For details about Google’s handling of data, see Google’s Privacy Policy.

Forms and question submissions

If you submit a question through the upload page, the form asks for your email address, question type, question text, and answer choices. If you ask a scorer a question, the form asks for your email address, an optional name or nickname, a topic, and your message so the site operator can reply. Both forms are handled by Formspree, a third-party form service, and submissions are sent to the site operator. Information you submit is subject to Formspree’s Privacy Policy as well as this page. Email sent directly to the contact address is also received by the site operator.

Referral rewards

If you join WoodpeckerSAT Rewards, your email, your generated referral code, and (if you used someone’s referral link) that referrer’s code are sent to the site operator through Formspree. Referred registrations also include a marker indicating that the referring person may qualify for the advertised $9.99-value gift. The gift is for the person who shared the link, not the person who registers through it. After a referred signup is confirmed, the site operator can match the referral code to the original registration and email the gift to the referrer. The QR code is generated in your browser and contains only your shareable referral URL, not your email address. A referral code from a link is temporarily kept in this browser’s session storage so it can be included if you register; your email address is not saved in browser storage. The referral code is removed from session storage after Formspree confirms the registration. The QR-code generator is loaded from cdnjs; it does not receive your email address.

There is no separate rewards database or automatic code-to-email lookup. To identify a referrer, the site operator can match the new registration’s referred_by_code to the original registration’s referral_code in Formspree. A referral is recorded only when Formspree confirms the new email registration, not when someone scans or opens a link. Codes are generated with secure browser randomness, but without a central registry the site cannot check for duplicates or guarantee global uniqueness. Gift emails are not sent automatically by the referral page; the site operator must match the confirmed referral in Formspree and send the gift to the referrer.
