
Step 1: Create a "Thank You" Page

You need a webpage that people only see after they do what you want them to do (like filling out a sign-up form or a contact form).
• Create a new HTML file called thank-you.html.
• Make your website's form automatically redirect users to this page when they click "Submit".
• Why this matters: If a user lands on this page, it proves they successfully signed up.

Step 2: Make Sure Google Analytics is on Every Page

Google Analytics can only track people if its code is on your pages.
• Open your HTML files in your code editor.
• Make sure your Google Analytics tracking script (the block of code they gave you) is pasted inside the <head> section of both your main page (index.html) and your new thank-you.html page.

Step 3: Tell Google to Track Sign-Ups

Now, tell Google Analytics that visiting the "Thank You" page is a big deal.
1. Log into your Google Analytics dashboard.
2. Click the gear icon (Admin) in the bottom left corner.
3. Under Data display, click on Events, then click Create event.
4. Name your event something like successful_signup.
5. Set the matching conditions so it triggers when the page_location contains /thank-you.html.
6. Save it. Finally, look at your events list and flip the switch to Mark as key event (this used to be called a conversion).

Step 4: Create the Special Links for Your Promoters

You cannot give all your promoters the same link. You must give them custom links so Google knows who sent who. Let's say you have a friend named Alex who wants to promote your site.
1. Go to Google's free Campaign URL Builder website.
2. Fill out the form exactly like this:
	• Website URL: https://yourwebsite.com
	• Campaign Source: alex (This is the promoter's name)
	• Campaign Medium: affiliate
	• Campaign Name: reward_program
3. Scroll down and copy the long link it generates for you. It will look like this:
https://yourwebsite.com
4. Send that exact long link to Alex. Tell them they must use this link when sharing your site.
(Repeat this step for your next promoter, just change the Campaign Source to their name, like sarah or mike).

Step 5: Check Who to Reward

When Alex and Sarah start sharing their links, people will visit your site and sign up. Here is how you see who brought them in:
1. Log into Google Analytics.
2. Go to Reports > Acquisition > Traffic acquisition.
3. In the table, look for a small blue + icon next to the words Session default channel group. Click it.
4. Go to Traffic source and select Session source/medium.
5. Look at the table. You will now see lines like alex / affiliate and sarah / affiliate.
6. Scroll all the way to the right side of that table to the Key events column. Look at the row for alex / affiliate. The number right there tells you exactly how many people Alex successfully got to sign up!
