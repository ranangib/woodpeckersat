I went from a 1300 to a 1500 on the SAT. And honestly, it wasn't easy.

I studied for 2 months. I thought I was ready. Then March came... 1300.

June went horribly. I struggled with consistency, discipline, and finding the motivation to actually sit down and study.

I genuinely thought I had fallen behind.

But after my August SAT was cancelled and I was given a makeup test, I decided I wasn't going to waste another opportunity.

This time, I approached studying differently.

📚 I completed 3,000–4,000 SAT practice questions in 2026

But the biggest difference wasn't the number of questions. It was HOW I practiced.

I started:

  • Reviewing every mistake instead of ignoring it.
  • Understanding WHY my answers were wrong.
  • Identifying my weakest topics.
  • Practicing consistently.
  • Focusing on improvement instead of just completing questions.

And in September, I finally got the score I had been working toward.

1300 → 1500.

One thing I learned along the way:

"It might sound simple, but in order to change your life, you must decide to actually do it."

You don't have to be naturally gifted. You don't have to have everything figured out. But you do need to be willing to put in the work and learn from your mistakes.
