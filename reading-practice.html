About SAT Reading & Writing Practice

The Digital SAT Reading and Writing section tests your ability to understand and analyze passages, interpret data, and apply standard English conventions. Questions are organized across four content domains:

  • Information and Ideas - Locate, interpret, and evaluate information from texts and informational graphics.
  • Craft and Structure - Determine word meanings, analyze text structure, and identify the author's purpose and perspective.
  • Expression of Ideas - Revise texts to improve clarity, effectiveness, and logical organization.
  • Standard English Conventions - Edit sentences for grammar, usage, and punctuation errors.

Each practice question includes detailed answer explanations so you can understand why the correct answer is right and why the other choices are wrong. Use the filters above to focus on a specific domain, skill, or difficulty level.

How to use this practice tool:

  1. Select a domain or skill from the filters to narrow the question bank.
  2. Click any question to open it and record your answer.
  3. Review the answer explanation carefully, even for questions you answered correctly.
  4. Bookmark questions you want to revisit using the printable study sheet.
  5. Your progress is saved locally in your browser - no account required.

For grammar and punctuation rules that appear in the Standard English Conventions domain, see the SAT Grammar Guide.
